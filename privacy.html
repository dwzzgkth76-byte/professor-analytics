Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Professor Analytics ("the app") handles information obtained through Google APIs.

What the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels (Professor Archive, Maple, Savvy and Econova), using the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API, after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission. This may include channel and video details, video metadata, and channel analytics and reports.

How the data is used

Data is used only to analyse and manage those channels, for example to review performance and to update the owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising or for any other purpose.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored privately and are used only by the app. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
